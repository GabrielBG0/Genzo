# WF-02 · Before / After (M1 part)

A simple before and after toggle in Develop. This spec covers what M1 builds (plan, M1 frontend item 7): `\` and the Before / After quick action switch the viewport between the photo's edit and its *before*. The full WF-02 comes in M3.

Sources: FEATURES.md (WF-02), the plan's M1 item 7 ("A simple before and after toggle (\\). The full WF-02 comes in M3"), APP-03 (the quick action and its *on* look), DEV-01 (*Generations*, the histogram), DEV-03, APP-10, OUT-01. Where this spec and the technical spec disagree, the technical spec wins and this file is corrected.

**Decided** (owner, 2026-11-07):
- `\` **toggles**: press it once for the before, again for the after. FEATURES.md's "\ hold before" comes with the full WF-02.
- **The before is the photo's defaults:** the default profile, white balance As Shot, every slider at 0, no crop or rotation. Edits live only for the session in M1, so nothing else could be the before. Once M2 keeps edits, the before is the state at import, and M3 lets the user choose it.

**Out of scope for M1**, with their milestone: side by side (Y), top and bottom (Alt Y), split (Shift Y), holding `\`, copying before to after and after to before, swapping, and choosing what the before is (M3, the full WF-02).

## Behavior

- **Before on:** the viewport shows the open photo rendered with its defaults instead of its edit. A **Before** badge shows at the viewport's top left, on the flat Overlay surface. The Before / After quick action shows as *on* (APP-03's toggle look: accent-soft text on its raised background).
- **Before off** (the after): the viewport shows the edit again.
- **Nothing in the edit changes:** turning the before on or off records no step (APP-10), changes no slider, and leaves the edited dots (DEV-13, LIB-09) as they were.
- **The panels keep the edit's values** while the before shows: they describe the edit, not the picture.
- **The histogram follows the picture** (DEV-03): it counts what the viewport shows, so the before's counts while it shows. The clipping overlays (J) follow it too.
- **Editing turns the before off.** A slider gesture's *start*, a typed value, a reset, Reset all, a choice, the white balance picker, Undo and Redo show the after again, with the change, in the same render. So nothing is ever changed out of sight.
- **A photo switch turns the before off.** The next photo opens showing its edit.
- **Zoom and the view** keep the before: a resize or a new view renders the before again.
- **Export exports the edit**, whichever shows (OUT-01).
- **During a gesture**, the toggle ends the gesture first (its step is recorded, APP-10), then shows the before.

### The engine's side

- `ShowBefore { photo, before }` turns the before on or off for the open photo and renders it settled. It answers `Edit`, the photo's edit with `before` set. For a photo that is not open it changes nothing and answers that photo's edit as it is.
- Each `PhotoEdit` the engine answers carries `before`: whether the viewport shows the before. The frontend draws the badge and the quick action from it.
- The before is held by the session, for the open photo only. Every command that changes the edit clears it before rendering; a switch clears it.

## Inputs and shortcuts

| Command | Shortcut | Also in |
|---|---|---|
| Before / after | `\` | Quick actions (APP-03); later the app menu (APP-02) and the photo's context menu (APP-06) |

- Registered in the shortcut registry as `develop.beforeAfter`, in Develop's scope, as it is now. The quick action's tooltip reads "Before / after  \".
- `\` in a text field types a backslash: the command does not run.

## States

| State | Command | What shows |
|---|---|---|
| No photo open, or the photo is unsupported or damaged | Disabled | — |
| After (default) | Enabled, off | The edit |
| Before | Enabled, on | The defaults, with the Before badge |
| The photo's first pixels are still coming (an embedded preview, DEV-01) | Enabled | The before renders when the photo's input comes, as every render does |

## Data

Nothing is stored. `before` lives in the engine's session for the open photo and goes with a switch.

## Edge cases

- **An unedited photo:** the before and the after are the same picture. The toggle still works and shows the badge, so the user can tell it did something.
- **Before on, then the user drags a slider:** the drag's *start* turns the before off; the drag's frames show the after.
- **Before on, then Undo:** the undo turns the before off and shows the after, undone.
- **Before on, then the window resizes, or zoom changes:** the before renders at the new view.
- **Before on, then Export:** the export has the edit, not the before.
- **A reply that arrives after a switch** (a `ShowBefore` sent before it): it changes nothing for the new photo.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **The before is the defaults:** with the before on, the settled frame equals, bit for bit, the frame of the same photo with a default edit (engine GPU test).
2. **Nothing recorded:** turning the before on and off leaves the edit, its history, `edited` and the revision as they were; Undo after it undoes the last real step (engine test).
3. **Editing turns it off:** with the before on, an `ApplyEditDelta`, a reset, Undo, Redo and a pick each answer `before: false`, and their frame is the after's (engine test).
4. **A switch turns it off** (engine test).
5. **The histogram is the before's** while it shows (engine test, against a default-edit render's counts).
6. **Export is the edit:** an export started with the before on is byte-identical to one started with it off (engine test).
7. **Frontend** (Vitest and Playwright against the fake engine): `\` and the quick action toggle it; the badge and the quick action's *on* look follow `before`; a slider drag turns it off; `\` in a text field types a backslash; the command is disabled with no photo open.
